My Story

For me, photography is more than just pressing a shutter button. It's about capturing moments when the light makes the world perfect for a split second. In my work, I focus on the untouched landscapes of Germany – from the misty forests in the North to the sun-drenched Alps in the South. I want to show that adventure is often waiting right on our doorstep."
